Instructions

  1. Select the testing data to predict its class, and then click the ADD button.
  2. Now, click on the NEXT button to initiate the training phase for the Naive Bayes model.
  3. Enter the occurrences of Yes(h) and No(-h) from the DATASET to calculate Prior Probabilities, then click SUBMIT.
  4. Click on the NEXT button to calculate Likelihood Probabilities for every word in the testing data.
  5. Input the number of times the word occurs in documents labeled with the specific class(Yes), and then click the SUBMIT button.
  6. Now, follow the same procedure for class No. Now, click on NEXT to proceed further.
    7. Note: If a zero probability is encountered, then apply Laplace smoothing.
  7. Calculate the Posterior Probability by entering the required values for P(h|d₇) and click on the SUBMIT button.
    • Click on the 'Prior Probability' tab given on the left side of the page to view the required values.
    • Next, click on the 'Likelihood Probability' tab and and observe the necessary values.
  8. Repeat the previous step(7) to calculate the Posterior Probability for P(-h|d₇).
  9. After submitting the values, click on the NEXT button to access the final result.
